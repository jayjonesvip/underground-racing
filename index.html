WINDOW BASICS

HOW TO BUILD A TICKET

  1. Pick the wager. Win means first; Place covers first or second; Show covers the top three.
  2. Pick the horse. Exacta requires two horses in exact finishing order. Exacta Box covers either order.
  3. Set the base wager. Box creates three bets—Win, Place, and Show. Exacta Box creates two combinations, so total cost multiplies.
  4. Check the ticket. The window always shows track, race, wager, selections, and total before you commit.

Trifecta and multi-race tickets are coming later. Start simple, read the form, and protect the wallet.
